Case Study One

JODAVI Media

Virtual Assistant & Sales Support · May 2025

About the Brand

JODAVI Media helps high-net-worth individuals and elite professionals build a stronger online presence through strategic storytelling, media visibility, and personal brand positioning.

I supported the founders with administrative coordination, prospect research, outreach, and sales support, helping organise the process of identifying and engaging potential clients.

The Challenge

Client acquisition required more than sending outreach messages. Prospects needed to be researched, conversations tracked, follow-ups managed, and meetings coordinated.

Without a structured approach, it could become difficult to maintain visibility over ongoing conversations, remember the next steps for each prospect, and coordinate meetings efficiently.

The goal was to support the founders with a more organised approach to prospecting, communication, and administrative coordination.

My Role & Responsibilities

1. Prospect Research & Lead Generation

I researched potential clients, including CEOs, founders, investors, and other professionals who could benefit from JODAVI Media’s services.

I built and maintained a targeted database of more than 200 prospects using Google Sheets and Notion.

My responsibilities included:

  • Identifying relevant prospects.
  • Researching and organising contact information.
  • Building and maintaining lead lists.
  • Supporting targeted LinkedIn and email outreach.
  • Tracking prospect responses and follow-up activities.

2. Outreach & Follow-Up Coordination

I supported communication with potential clients through LinkedIn and email.

Beyond initiating conversations, I tracked responses and maintained follow-ups to help ensure interested prospects were not overlooked.

This involved:

  • Preparing and sending outreach messages.
  • Monitoring prospect responses.
  • Organising follow-up activities.
  • Maintaining clear records of ongoing conversations.
  • Supporting the progression of prospects towards introductory calls.

3. Administrative & Meeting Coordination

I also supported the founders with administrative tasks connected to prospect engagement and client acquisition.

This included:

  • Scheduling calls and meetings.
  • Coordinating physical executive interviews.
  • Communicating meeting details and arrangements.
  • Updating prospect records.
  • Supporting client onboarding and related follow-up activities.

My role helped connect the outreach process with the administrative tasks required to keep conversations and meetings moving.

4. Results & Contributions

My work contributed to a more organised prospecting and follow-up process.

Key contributions included:

  • Built and maintained a database of more than 200 targeted prospects.
  • Booked an average of 5 to 6 qualified calls per month.
  • Contributed to the acquisition of two high-value clients within approximately 10 to 14 days.
  • Supported meeting scheduling, prospect communication, and onboarding coordination.
  • Helped maintain visibility over prospect interactions and outstanding follow-ups.

The client acquisition outcomes were team results supported by my contribution to prospecting and outreach, rather than deals I independently closed.

5. Key Takeaway

This experience strengthened my ability to combine administrative support with business development activities.

It taught me the importance of organised records, timely communication, consistent follow-ups, and reliable coordination in helping a business maintain its client acquisition process.

These are the same skills I bring to virtual assistance and administrative support roles: keeping information organised, managing details, and ensuring important tasks do not fall through the cracks.

  • Prospect Research
  • Records Management
  • Communication
  • Follow-Up Coordination
  • Calendar & Meeting Support
  • CRM and Lead Tracking
  • Client Onboarding Support

Case Study Two

YouNick Studio

Brand Development Lead & Outreach Manager · Jun to Jul 2024

About the Brand

YouNick Studio is a creative brand identity and design business that helps businesses communicate their identity through branding and visual design services.

My role combined prospect research, outreach, content coordination, and the preparation of business materials to support the studio’s visibility and client acquisition efforts.

The Challenge

As a growing creative business, YouNick Studio needed a structured way to identify potential clients, communicate the value of its services, and maintain consistent outreach.

It was also important to organise marketing activities and supporting materials so that prospecting, content planning, and follow-ups could work together rather than operate as disconnected tasks.

My focus was to support these activities through research, organisation, communication, and practical execution.

My Role & Responsibilities

1. Lead Research & Outreach

I researched potential clients who could benefit from brand identity and design services.

Within one month, I organised more than 200 qualified prospects in Google Sheets.

My responsibilities included:

  • Researching businesses that could benefit from branding services.
  • Identifying and organising potential clients.
  • Supporting outreach and prospect engagement.
  • Tracking leads and follow-up activities.
  • Helping communicate the value of the studio’s services to prospective clients.

2. Content Planning & Engagement

I supported the studio’s Instagram content planning and engagement activities.

This involved organising content ideas, planning communication, and supporting a consistent brand presence.

I also worked on comment and engagement strategies to help the brand participate in relevant conversations and improve how it connected with its audience.

My responsibilities included:

  • Coordinating Instagram content planning.
  • Organising content ideas and publishing activities.
  • Supporting audience engagement.
  • Developing comment and engagement strategies.
  • Helping maintain consistency in the way the brand communicated its services.

3. Business Documentation & Brand Positioning

I supported the preparation of business materials that helped communicate the studio’s services and present its offers professionally.

This included assisting with pricing sheets, proposals, and Instagram highlights.

The work required attention to detail, clear communication, and an understanding of how to present information in a way that prospective clients could understand.

4. Lead Tracking & Follow-Up Organisation

I helped organise lead information and outreach activities into a more structured tracking process.

By keeping prospect information and follow-up activities organised, the team could maintain a clearer view of ongoing outreach and the next actions required. The lead tracker shown above is the supporting evidence for this work.

5. Results & Contributions

My contributions included:

  • Researched and organised more than 200 qualified prospects within one month.
  • Supported prospect outreach and lead organisation.
  • Coordinated Instagram content planning and engagement activities.
  • Developed a comment strategy calendar to structure audience engagement.
  • Assisted with proposals, pricing materials, and Instagram highlights.
  • Helped establish a more organised approach to lead tracking and follow-up.

These contributions brought together business development, communication, content coordination, and administrative organisation.

6. Key Takeaway

My experience at YouNick Studio showed me how administrative organisation and communication support wider business objectives.

Whether I was organising prospect information, preparing a proposal, or coordinating a content calendar, the underlying goal was the same: make information easier to manage, keep activities organised, and help the business execute its plans more effectively.

These experiences are directly relevant to administrative and virtual assistant roles that require research, documentation, communication, and consistent follow-through.

  • Lead Tracking
  • Business Communication
  • Document Preparation
  • Content Coordination
  • Engagement Planning
  • Follow-Up Management
